// The Problem

What Actually Stops People At Deal One

Advice is everywhere and none of it is local

The strategy that works in a coastal market doesn't survive contact with Oklahoma pricing, Oklahoma rents, or Oklahoma programs. Local is the only version that matters to you.

You've read enough

Reading more is the thing that feels like progress. Another podcast, another thread, another course — none of it is the step you haven't taken, and some of it is a way of not taking it.

You've never met anyone who's done it

The fastest way past deal one is proximity to people on deal fifty — close enough to ask the small question you would never post publicly.

// The Evidence

Why Oklahoma City, In Three Numbers

You don't need a thesis to start. You do need to understand the market you're starting in.

+13,700

Metro residents added in a year, 0.9% growth. More people is the demand side of every strategy you might pick.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, Vintage 2025 estimates · accessed Aug 27, 2026
$316,450

Median home listing price in the OKC metro, July 2026 — a low entry price, which matters more on your first deal than on any deal after it.

Source: Realtor.com listing data, July 2026 · accessed Aug 27, 2026
41%

Of Oklahoma City households rent — the tenant pool behind any buy-and-hold strategy you're considering.
