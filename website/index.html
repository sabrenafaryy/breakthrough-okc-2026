Why The Map Is The Whole Game

30 programs

Public funding programs across the city, state and federal layers in Oklahoma. Different rules, different cycles, different qualification bars. There is no single place anybody hands you that map — which is why we wrote one, and it comes with your seat.

Seven

How many of the state’s housing finance programs Darrell Beavers administers at the Oklahoma Housing Finance Agency. On September 26 he walks the room through all of them and takes live questions.

Since 1991

How long he has been at OHFA, with 48,000+ affordable housing units financed through the programs he administers. He is also an active real estate broker and investor — which is why he explains this in deal terms rather than agency terms.
