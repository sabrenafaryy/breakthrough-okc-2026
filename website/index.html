// The Money Is Funded. The Line Is Short.

Why The Map Is The Whole Game

6 people

That's how many taxpayers in the entire state claimed the historic rehab credit in 2023, the most recent full year reported — a credit worth up to 40% of rehab costs. The money is enormous. The line is six people long.

Source: Oklahoma Incentive Evaluation Commission, Oct 2024 · accessed Aug 25, 2026
$39.7M

Sitting unallocated in the NE Renaissance TIF budget per the city's own FY24 report — including a $5M residential line that has never paid out a dollar. The district is funded. The applicants are missing.

Source: City of OKC FY24 TIF Annual Report, as of June 30, 2024 · accessed Aug 25, 2026
0% interest

What the state charges homebuilders for construction money under the Housing Stability Program — no income restriction on your buyers, and 75% of the pool reserved for rural projects. No bank will match it, because no bank can.

Source: OHFA Housing Stability Program, Jan 2026 fact sheets · accessed Aug 25, 2026
